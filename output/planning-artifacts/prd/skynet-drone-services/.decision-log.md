# Decision Log

All major architectural, product, and scope decisions for the Skynet Drone Services PRD will be tracked here.

## 2026-05-24
- **Working Mode:** Coaching Path -> Personas & Journeys entry point.
- **Stakes:** High (Prototype scaling to full commercial launch).
- **Core Model:** B2B2C Marketplace Aggregator (Drone-as-a-Service), targeting smallholder farmers via FPOs and CHCs.
- **Architecture:** Tenant Hierarchy (Parent = View/Aggregate, Child = Operational/Booking) to support large estates without accidental bulk-booking risks.
- **Data Integration:** Mandatory integration with government land registries to validate farm boundaries.
- **Accessibility UX:** Dropped the complex "Tamil Voice UI Booking" feature in favor of a direct Telephony Helpline. Digital bookings are for managers/estate owners, while traditional smallholder farmers will be routed through phone agents for maximum conversion and zero digital friction.
- **Product Strategy (AgriTwin insight):** Position Skynet as the physical execution layer (Micro) triggered by external satellite data (Macro). 
- **Architecture (Map Layers):** Added WMS/WMTS ingestion so we can offer advanced remote-sensing indices without buying expensive multispectral drone cameras.
- **Capability Tiers:** Phased rollout agreed: Tier 1 (2D Orthomosaics via ODM), Tier 2 (DEM/3D Mesh), Tier 3 (LiDAR pipeline as a future discrete addition).
- **Processing Architecture:** Mandated async, event-driven pipeline (Queue → Object Storage) to cleanly handle large photogrammetry jobs and prepare the seam for future LiDAR processing without rewriting the core.
- **Finalized:** 2026-06-04: PRD status set to final after concluding capability tiers and strategic technical discussions.
