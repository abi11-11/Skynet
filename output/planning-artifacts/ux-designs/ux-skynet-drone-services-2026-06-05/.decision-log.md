# UX Decision Log
*Date: 2026-06-05*

## Discovery Intake
- **Sources Extracted:** `prd.md`, `architecture.md`, `epics.md`
- **Foundation System:** Google Material 3 (M3)
- **Design Inspiration:** Industry-standard geospatial & ag-tech applications (e.g., DJI Pilot, DroneDeploy, OnX Hunt) focusing on the "Glass Cockpit" approach, high-contrast sunlight readability, large touch targets for gloved hands, and "Telescoping" (progressive disclosure) UI layers.

## Core UX Directives (From PRD)
- **Map-Centric Interface:** The map is the primary navigation surface. 
- **Role-Based Contexts:** 
  - *Farm Manager/Caretaker:* Needs task-oriented, proactive booking prompts right on the plot cards.
  - *Estate Owner:* Needs a high-level "Telescope View" with traffic-light color coding (Estate -> Plot).
  - *Drone Pilot:* Needs clear boundaries, offline-first sync indicators, and real-time RTK telemetry overlays that don't obstruct the map.
- **Offline & Environment:** Explicit visual states for offline modes. Needs high-contrast modes for bright sunlight.

## Visual Identity (DESIGN.md overrides)
- **Theme/Vibe:** "The Agronomist" (Growth & Health)
- **Colors:** Vibrant Leaf Green primary, with Warm Off-Whites / Deep Soil Browns for dark mode backgrounds. UI should feel organic but modern, focused on nurturing crops.
- **Typography:** `Roboto` (Standard, familiar Android/Google feel, highly legible).
## Interaction Patterns (EXPERIENCE.md overrides)
- **Primary Paradigm:** The "Glass Cockpit". The map is always the anchor.
- **Booking Flow:** Draggable Bottom Sheet with Snap Detents (M3 standard). The sheet opens at 50% height over the map for context, but can be swiped up to 100% full screen to reveal complex forms or additional fields. This perfectly balances spatial context with required vertical real estate.
